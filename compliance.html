Regulatory Framework

The Laws That Shape Our Practice

A plain-language view of the primary federal standards we operate under and how each one is reflected in daily procedures.

Regulations and how Equitas applies them
Regulation What it governs How we apply it
FDCPA Conduct of third-party debt collectors, including contact times, harassment, and debt validation. Statutory calling windows, written validation notices, and a zero-tolerance policy on abusive practices.
FCRA Accuracy and fairness of information used in consumer credit reporting. Balance verification before placement and documented investigation of disputed information.
TCPA Rules for automated and prerecorded calls and text messages. Consent-aware dialing, honored opt-outs, and automated caller ID identification.
GLBA Safeguarding of nonpublic personal financial information. Encryption, least-privilege access, and controlled retention of account data.
HIPAA Privacy and security of protected health information. Minimum-necessary handling and restricted access for healthcare-related accounts.

State and local requirements may also apply and are reviewed for each placement.

Consumer Protection

Communication Standards

We uphold the standards established under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), and Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA). Three practices define how every consumer conversation is handled.

Permissible Contact Windows

Outreach is restricted to statutory contact hours (8:00 AM – 9:00 PM in the account holder’s local time zone), with frequency limits that prevent repetitive calls.

Know Your Rights

Your Rights at a Glance

Account holders are entitled to clear information and respectful treatment at every step. If you ever feel those standards are not being met, tell us directly.

Contact the Compliance Desk
  1. Request validation. Within 30 days of our first written notice, you may ask for verification of the debt and the name of the original creditor.
  2. Dispute in writing. If you dispute the debt within that window, collection of the disputed amount pauses until we provide verification.
  3. Choose how we contact you. You may tell us in writing to stop contact or to limit it, including contact at your workplace.
  4. Be treated with respect. Threats, harassment, obscene language, and false statements are prohibited.
  5. Receive clear information. Balances, fees, and creditor identity are disclosed in plain language.

Information Security

Data Security, Documentation & Case Records

Protecting confidential debtor and client data is fundamental to our practice. We enforce multi-layer technical safeguards and strict documentation hygiene.

  • AES-256 Encrypted InfrastructureClient files and debtor communications are encrypted at rest and in transit.
  • Role-Based Access ControlsLeast-privilege permissions keep sensitive account records limited to authorized officers.
  • Secure File TransferPlacement files move through encrypted channels and are scrubbed before any outreach begins.
  • Audit Retention & ReleaseSettlement receipts and payment logs are archived, and paid-in-full release documents are delivered promptly.

Common Questions

Compliance FAQs

Quick answers for account holders and clients.

How do I dispute a debt?

Send a written dispute within 30 days of receiving our first notice, using the form below or by mail. Collection of the disputed portion is paused until we provide verification.

Can I ask you to stop contacting me?

Yes. Send a written request and we will stop contact, except for limited notices that the law permits, such as confirming the request or advising of a specific action.

Are calls recorded?

Calls are recorded for quality assurance and compliance review, and you will be informed as required by applicable law.

How is my personal information protected?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is limited by role, and records are retained and released under documented procedures.

Who do I contact with a complaint?

Reach the Compliance Desk at 1-800-555-0199 or compliance@equitasrecovery.com, or use the inquiry form below. Every complaint is logged and reviewed.
