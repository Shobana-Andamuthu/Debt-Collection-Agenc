Governance & Consumer Protection

Statutory adherence, transparent case handling, and respectful communication that protect consumer rights throughout recovery.

Ethical Charter

Our Core Governance Principles

Every interaction is guided by clear ethical boundaries, mutual respect, and legal integrity.

Dignity & Respect

We treat every account holder with dignity. We strictly prohibit threatening language, high pressure, or abusive tactics.

Full Transparency

Plain-language disclosures of outstanding balances, principal amounts, and fees with zero concealed or inflated charges.

Hardship Consideration

Fair evaluation of genuine financial hardship to establish workable repayment arrangements that prevent recurring default.

Strict Accountability

Comprehensive call recording and time-stamped case logs to ensure complete audit readiness and adherence to policy.

Consumer Rights

Communication Standards & Consumer Protection

We uphold strict standards established under the Fair Debt Collection Practices Act (FDCPA), Fair Credit Reporting Act (FCRA), and Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA).

Permissible Contact Windows

Outreach is restricted exclusively to statutory contact hours (8:00 AM – 9:00 PM in the debtor's local time zone) with frequency caps preventing repetitive calls.

  • Respectful call frequency limits
  • Strict workplace contact opt-out honoring
  • Automated caller ID identification

Written Debt Validation (30-Day Notice)

Every initial outreach is followed by a formal written notice detailing the original creditor, balance breakdown, and statutory 30-day validation rights.

  • Clear 30-day dispute window instructions
  • Original creditor identification
  • Itemized principal and interest documentation

Formal Dispute Handling

If a debt is disputed in writing, collection activities on the disputed portion are promptly paused while our compliance unit verifies documentation with the original creditor.

  • Immediate outreach pause upon notice
  • Independent review by compliance officers
  • Written verification and updated reporting

Regulatory Notice: The information provided on this page outlines Equitas Recovery Partners’ operational compliance practices and general consumer protection policies. It is provided for informational transparency and does not constitute formal legal advice. Consumers seeking legal representation should consult a qualified attorney.

Information Security

Data Security, Documentation & Case Records

Protecting confidential debtor and client data is fundamental to our practice. We enforce multi-layer technical safeguards and strict documentation hygiene.

AES-256 Encrypted Infrastructure

All client files and debtor communications are protected with end-to-end AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit.

Role-Based Access Controls

Strict least-privilege security permissions ensure sensitive account records are accessible only by authorized officers.

Permanent Audit Retention & Release

Complete archival of settlement receipts, payment logs, and prompt delivery of paid-in-full release documentation.
