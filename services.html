Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about placing accounts with Equitas.

What types of accounts can we place with you?

We accept consumer and commercial receivables, including utilities, healthcare, rental, trade invoices, and contract defaults. Early-stage accounts that are 30–60 days past due can also be enrolled in Account & Ledger Care.

How does onboarding work?

After a confidential portfolio review, you send placement files through an encrypted transfer. We scrub the data for compliance, validate balances, and confirm the outreach plan with you before any contact begins.

How do you protect our reputation with customers?

Scripts and notices are written in plain language, calls are recorded, and prohibited tactics are not used. Tone and sequencing for sensitive accounts are agreed with you in advance.

How quickly are collected funds remitted?

Collected funds are remitted electronically on the schedule set in your agreement, with an itemized statement that reconciles each payment to the account ledger.

What happens when an account holder disputes a balance?

Collection on the disputed portion is paused while our compliance team verifies documentation with you. The account holder receives written verification, and the result is recorded in your portal. Read the dispute process.
