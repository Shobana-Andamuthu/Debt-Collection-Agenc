Fee FAQs

Frequently Asked Pricing Questions

Clear answers regarding our billing principles, remittance cycles, and fee transparency.

How are contingency percentage rates determined?

Contingency rates are structured based on account aging, placement volume, and balance size. Rates are confirmed in writing prior to placement with zero surprise changes.

Are there any hidden administrative or onboarding charges?

No. We operate with strict fee transparency. Standard contingency placements involve zero setup, onboarding, or skip tracing surcharges.

How quickly are recovered funds disbursed to our company?

Collected funds are electronically remitted on an agreed schedule (typically bi-weekly or monthly) alongside itemized statement ledgers.

What happens if an assigned account cannot be collected?

On our standard contingency plan, if no funds are recovered from an account, you owe zero collection fees. You only pay when we deliver results.
